# PanoMetal54 mobile design

Primary users visit on phones. The default CSS starts at 320 px and expands to tablet and desktop; mobile is the primary composition.

## Identity
A simple cobalt panel/drop symbol with an upright charcoal Sayaç Kapak wordmark and PanoMetal54 endorsement. No meter illustration or tiny dial. The PNG is generated artwork, not a claimed vector master. Optimized PNG and favicon are included in public/images/brand.

## Visual system
Warm off-white canvas, charcoal text and industrial surfaces, cobalt actions. Restrained directional shadows, inset product switch, consistent 8/12 px component radii, 20 px mobile gutters. Native system sans with deliberate type scale and limited line lengths. No idle animation, glow, scrolling spectacle, fake metrics or testimonials.

## Mobile journeys
The home page exposes exactly two products through a keyboard-accessible tab switch. Product selection carries into the enquiry sheet. The fixed bottom bar offers phone and WhatsApp actions, with a safe-area inset. The sheet opens from the bottom on phones; optional measurements are collapsed initially. Input font sizes remain 16 px. No submitted data is stored; the form prepares a WhatsApp message for the customer to send.

Both product pages use the same mobile typography, contact actions and product navigation. Real water-cover photographs remain in the gallery. No generated gas-panel photo is presented as actual work.

## Motion and accessibility
Small press feedback and 220 ms sheet/product transitions communicate actions. Reduced motion removes transitions. Native dialogs provide focus containment and Escape dismissal. Product tabs support arrows, Home and End, and expose selection state. Visible focus and descriptive image alternatives remain.

## Search
Three server-rendered Turkish pages, product-specific title/description/canonical, local business and service data, robots and sitemap. GitHub Pages deploys main /docs. Do not point canonical URLs to an unpurchased domain.
